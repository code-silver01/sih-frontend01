THE PROBLEM

A temperature reading isn't enough.

Cold-chain failures can happen between checkpoints. Traditional monitoring can tell you what happened — but not whether the record itself can be trusted.

01

Silent excursions

Temperature and humidity can drift outside acceptable conditions without immediate human intervention.

02

Disconnected journeys

Connectivity isn't guaranteed throughout transport. Critical telemetry must survive network outages.

03

Questionable records

A database can store a number. It doesn't inherently prove that the number wasn't modified later.

Intelligence at the edge. Integrity all the way through.
